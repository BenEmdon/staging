Experiment staging

Previews for benemdon.github.io. Everything here is an experiment under review, not the live site, and nothing here is indexed.

Each experiment lives in a folder named after it: branch experiment/<name> maps to staging/<name>/. On Ben's ship call, the verified build here is promoted to the live site.

  • blog/

    Blog preview: six posts, an index, and an Atom feed. Parked while its markdown formatting gets a consistency pass.

  • dither-installation/

    Dither installation, reimagined: two layered dithered scenes on the art page, Parliament Hill and the Rideau Canal, under a cover that clears in stepped blocks.

  • hiring-reveal/

    Hiring scene reveal: on hover, a tiny meadow-echo scene (field, hill, tower, blinking lamp) assembles cell by cell in the hiring block's base margin and drains on leave.

  • hiring-bloom/

    Hiring bloom: pointer-reactive pixel clusters in the home page hiring block. Where the pointer lingers, Bayer-cluster blooms ignite in stepped shades and grow cluster by cluster; a fast pass scatters a few single cells, and everything steps back down in place when the pointer leaves.

  • hiring-wake/

    Hiring wake: the home page hiring block's hover becomes a stepped pixel ripple. A still dither field fills the block; the pointer sends rings of solid shades expanding from its path, wider and brighter the faster it moves, then stepping back down in place.

  • wordmark-letters/

    Wordmark letters: the letter under the pointer fills with accent cells in seeded Bayer order and drains on leave, so a sweep lights BEN EMDON letter by letter instead of slicing columns through the letterforms.

  • wordmark-pool/

    Wordmark pool: a pool of light travels across the BEN EMDON wordmark with the pointer. Cells near the pointer take the brightest rung of a three-step shade ladder, stepping down with distance, so the light crosses letter boundaries in steps instead of slicing single columns. Slow movement keeps a tight pool; a fast flick stretches it along the travel direction.

The live site is at benemdon.github.io.
